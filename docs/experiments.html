E2 — bias-matched instrument (diagnostic)

Identical geometry and scoring, but each eligible segment's withholding probability is proportional to (1 − mean e(x))², i.e. the withheld set is enriched where the catalogue is sparse — the situation the lane exists to correct.

SAR 0.020985 vs nnPU 0.020816; paired Δ = +1.70e−04, 95 % CI [−9.3e−05, +4.1e−04]. Top tercile SAR 0.01731 vs nnPU 0.01729.

Why the effect is small (diagnosed, not excused)

The fitted propensity barely varies among labelled positives (mean 0.934, min 0.792, max 0.994), so no inverse weight reached the clip at 10 and the correction is close to inert. This is the identifiability limit the protocol predicts for PU data: the scale of e(x) is not pinned down without an external completeness anchor, and a flat fit yields flat weights.

E3 — the scale bug, the fix, and the honest result

A unit audit of the EM recursion found the propensity degeneracy was mathematical, not geological: the labelled set is the complete catalogue while the unlabeled set is a 1/127.7 subsample of the pool, and the published code compared them without a sampling-fraction weight. The implied hidden-positive mass was therefore 127.7× too small, and the EM fixed point was pinned near e(x) ≈ 1 whatever the covariates said. The corrected recursion runs on pixel-population counts:

n_pos(c) = labelled_ratio·n_S(c) + pool_ratio·pi·n_U(c)·e(c)      e(c) = n_S(c)/n_pos(c)

After the fix, in the scoring folds the fitted propensity is non-degenerate: epos ∈ [0.436, 0.720], sd 0.055, pool ratio 127.7, clip fraction 0.0000. In the whole-catalogue fit used for the published raster the inverse weights vary with mean 1.604, max 2.282, and a clipped fraction of 0.0000 — i.e. for the first time the correction is actually doing something.

Interpretation. Uniform segment withholding makes the withheld positives exchangeable with the visible ones, so the instrument contains no selection bias for the correction to remove. A tie here is the expected outcome and is the control condition, not evidence against the mechanism.
