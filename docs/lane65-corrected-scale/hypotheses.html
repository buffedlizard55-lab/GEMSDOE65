1Metric-geometry-matched emission (new top priority) No new layer: the same SAR probability surface, re-emitted. Signature is a placement rule, not a detector: accept a dot only where the kernel credit exceeds the plug-in bar k > α·DTI, restrict the allowed set to a 10–40 px dilation of the visible catalogue, and set the assumed truth mass from the family's OWNER-REPORT hidden-mass estimate K̂ ≈ 12 226 px instead of π·n_active. The metric pays only for mass near true faults; a dot 1.2 km away earns ≈ 0 credit but still costs 0.2 per unit. This session's candidate sits a median of 12.5 px from the catalogue and emits 28 047 dots — more mass than the estimated hidden truth — which mathematically caps the score. Every previous arm in this lane (and the family's PU variants) tuned the surface and kept the template's fixed-budget allocator. Nobody has audited the emission budget against the metric's marginal algebra with the family's own K̂. Low — one allocator option plus a re-run of the existing folds.
2Non-degenerate SAR-EM propensity (implemented and fixed here) All 19 official bands as context, plus the five registered propensity covariates (relief, slope, depth_to_base_surf, survey coverage, distance to range front); signature is the selection surface, not a fault detector. Re-weights training towards terrain where mapping was sparse, so the learned fault template is not dominated by easy-to-see range-front scarps (the brief's 92 %/25 % observation). The family's PU arm (20GEMSDOE h20) used nnPU with a fixed prior π = 0.0363 and no propensity model; this session's E1/E2 used a 4-bin quantile grid whose fit was flat. A continuous logistic SAR-EM with an anchored scale has not been tried. Low — code exists here; needs an anchor or an explicit sensitivity band.
3Buried-fault wedges in basin-fill thickness Official depth_to_base_surf (gravity-derived basin fill), iso_grav_anom and its slope, plus detrended elevation; signature = along-strike gradient / curvature ridges of fill thickness and second-derivative structure in the isostatic gravity field. The catalogue is surface-mapping-biased; faults under basin fill have no scarp, but they do change the fill-thickness field. Those pixels are systematically the omitted ones. Previous work fed gravity edge products (grav_edge, depth_grad) into classifiers; a structural-geology model of fill-thickness gradient zones conditioned on range-front geometry has not been built here. Medium; the needed layers are already in the official feature stack (verify band metadata first).
4Radiometric alteration haloes near fault intersections USGS GeoDAWN airborne radiometric K/Th/U equivalents (public release) plus official cond_surf; signature = potassium-enrichment / radiometric-low haloes aligned along fault strike at intersections. Geothermal upflow alters rock along faults and that alteration can persist after the scarp has degraded; the prize cares specifically about geothermal-indicative faults. The family's prior work is scarp-first: GEMSDOE46 r11f/r12 fused radiometrics OWNER-REPORT with scarp detections, and its registry states explicitly that no arm uses the precomputed Th/K band. This hypothesis inverts the order — ratio-edge-first, scarp-free — and conditions the anomaly on strike and intersection density. Medium; 4-band GeoDAWN radiometrics (K/Th/U/TC) exist as a pinned pack in the family's public data mirror (data/external/geodawn_rad_u8.tif). Free and official; no download was performed this session.
5Acquisition-date natural experiment (lidar after mapping) Lidar/2 m scarp channels plus the NGMDB publication dates; signature = high scarp SNR in areas whose lidar postdates the published map that had no fault there. Turns the visibility bias into an instrument: a scarp that exists in modern lidar but not on the older published map is a candidate miss with a measurable explanation. The family's h66 lane treated lidar scarplets as a detector; nobody has used map publication date as the discriminating variable. High; needs NGMDB map-date metadata — free and public, but its join to the grid is unverified here.
6Structural-strike-constrained fluid-discharge lines depth_to_base_surf + cond_surf + free USGS/GDR spring and well inventories; signature = discharge points aligned along a common strike, with evaporative playa margins explicitly masked out (the named mimic). Unmapped faults are the ones that still control fluid discharge but were never drawn; a strike-alignment test rejects the playa-margin mimic that defeated earlier habitat attempts. This habitat has already been audited quantitatively by the family: 70.04 % of the 62 703 sgmc_off_catalogue pixels beyond 300 m of the catalogue lie in elevated mountain blocks (median +127.36 m relief, median depth-to-base 91.74 m), and its off-catalogue calibration arm sgmc_prevalence_calibrated_dti_44090px scored 0.00418 on the catalogue instrument (HOLDOUT-DTI, family registry) — the weakest of five. What is untried is the strike constraint plus an explicit playa-margin mask. Medium; NWIS/GDR inventories are free official sources — obtainable.
