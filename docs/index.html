Selection-bias-aware fault discovery: what the evidence supports, and what it does not.

The supplied fault catalogue is a selected-at-random sample of the real fault population, conditioned on observables. This project estimates that selection propensity with SAR-EM (Bekker & Davis, PMLR 94, 2018) from five observability covariates, trains the fault posterior with the propensity-aware empirical risk of Bekker, Robberechts & Davis (arXiv:1809.03207) with weights clipped at 10, and compares it against an nnPU baseline on identical spatially blocked folds.

Sensitivity: partial propensity correction

Full correction (weight 1/e) improved the pooled blocked-holdout DTI but pushed the emission out of the well-mapped, high-observability terrain where the withheld catalogue segments actually live, so the highest-propensity tercile collapsed. Shrinking the weights toward 1, w = 1 / e^lambda, interpolates between the nnPU baseline (λ = 0) and the full propensity-aware risk (λ = 1).
