Executive summary

One-paragraph version. This lane treats the competition's fault catalogue as positives selected with a covariate-dependent probability rather than a random sample of all faults. Two independent public inventories of the same ground (the USGS QFaults/INGENIOUS compilation that is the catalogue — 96.5 % coverage at 0 px median distance — and a state geologic map fault layer that overlaps it by only 4.5 %) make the selection bias measurable: the catalogue over-represents deep-basin-fill, low-relief ground, the state maps over-represent exposed high-relief ground. The propensity e(x) is anchored on that differential, the classifier is trained with a propensity-aware risk, and dots are placed where P(fault|x)·(1 − e(x)) is largest — i.e. where a fault is likely and the catalogue is blind.
