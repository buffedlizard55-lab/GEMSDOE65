2. Parallel-run protocol: duplicate audit against every published lane

Rule: stop as a duplicate if the rank correlation with any registry raster exceeds 0.90, or if more than 70% of the dots fall within 3 px of a single registry raster's dots. Both readings come from the frozen template tool and are applied twice — once to the score surface before placement, once to the final dots.

3. What a green banner here does and does not mean

  • It means: the file is on the pinned grid, all-finite, in [0, 1], positive only outside the mapped catalogue, and not a copy of a published lane.
  • It does not mean: organizer acceptance, a leaderboard score, or a reserved submission slot. Promotion to a slot is a separate selector decision, within the weekly cap.
