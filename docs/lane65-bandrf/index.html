3 · Why this method, in one paragraph

Fault catalogues are a selected sample: a step-over is mapped when a mapper is already working that system, when the substrate and light allow it to be seen, and when mapping effort reached it — published mapping in this region reports that about 92 % of step-overs sit on range-front faults and only about a quarter are mapped at ≈ 1 : 24 000. Treating “unlabeled” as “negative” therefore trains on mapper attention as much as on geology. This lane models the inclusion probability e(x) = P(labeled | fault, x) with the SAR-EM estimator of Bekker & Davis (2018) over a five-covariate set (local relief, slope, gravity-derived basin-fill proxy, lidar coverage, distance to the morphometric range front) and trains with the propensity-weighted empirical risk of Bekker, Robberechts & Davis (2019), inverse-propensity weights clipped at 10, against a matched nnPU baseline on identical folds. The emission rule that the hold-out actually selects is the measured splay band of the visible catalogue (inter-trace spacing) crossed with range-front proximity — an inverse-selection reweighting that puts mass where the missing population lives (a measured 0.4–2 km stand-off from the mapped traces), not in a halo hugging them. 4 · Limits, stated plainly

  • The propensity is not anchored to an index of exhaustively mapped quadrangles (none was found), so it is reported as a sensitivity analysis, not as a calibrated probability: PU data alone cannot identify the labelling mechanism.
  • The blocked hold-out is deliberately harder than the competition task; a HOLDOUT-DTI value from it is not a leaderboard forecast, and no projection is ever written as a score here.
  • The three valid footprint masks in the mirrored inputs disagree by up to 1 521 cells (feature stack 5 165 852 · sample template 5 167 373 · DEM 5 167 187). This project uses whichever mask the shared grid returns and never mixes them; the discrepancy is logged as an irregularity.
  • Inputs are integrity-pinned mirrors, not organizer-authenticated downloads; the pins, sizes and SHA-256 values are in evidence/input_pins.json.
  • Everything this lane found irregular is itemised, with its evidence and disposition, in IRREGULARITIES.md (lane entry IR-65-025).
