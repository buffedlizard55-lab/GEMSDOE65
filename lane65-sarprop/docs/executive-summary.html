What this file is

Lane Selection-bias-aware PU learning. The catalogue is treated as a sample of the truth, not as the truth.

Model Propensity-weighted risk (Bekker, Robberechts & Davis, arXiv:1809.03207) with the sampling propensity estimated by the EM method of Bekker & Davis, PMLR v94. Propensity attributes: local relief, slope, gravity-derived basin-fill proxy, lidar coverage, distance to range front. Classifier design: the 19 official bands plus those 5 covariates.

Baseline it must beat nnPU on identical folds — same draw, same design, same emission rule.

Gate strictly higher pooled HOLDOUT-DTI and no loss in the highest-propensity tercile — stated before the number was read. Verdict: PROMOTE (overall delta 0.005378, tercile delta 0.011799).

Emitted 74351 dots of value 1.0, forced to be more than 3 px apart (non-maximum suppression at the metric's own kernel radius), then thinned by the measured ≤2 px prune rule; zero outside the competition footprint and zero on every mapped fault.
